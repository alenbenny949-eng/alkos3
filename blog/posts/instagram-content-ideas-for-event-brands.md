Event work is visual, so Instagram is a natural showroom. The challenge is staying consistent.

## A simple weekly rhythm
- **Monday:** a finished event highlight (reel or carousel)
- **Wednesday:** behind-the-scenes of setup or planning
- **Friday:** client moment or review screenshot
- **Sunday:** a tip, checklist or idea for future clients

## Keep it working for you
1. Put your booking link in your bio.
2. Save your best posts as Highlights (Venues, Decor, Reviews).
3. Reply to comments and DMs quickly.
