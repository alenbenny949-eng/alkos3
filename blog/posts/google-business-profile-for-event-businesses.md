People searching for an event planner, decorator or photographer usually start on Google Maps. Your Google Business Profile is often the first impression you make.

## 1. Choose the right categories
Pick one primary category that matches your main service, then add secondary ones for everything else you offer.

## 2. Upload real event photos
Add fresh photos of real events every month: stage setups, decor, venues and behind-the-scenes shots.

## 3. Ask for reviews after every event
Send a short message with your review link while the experience is still fresh. Reply to every review, good or bad.

## 4. Keep hours and contact details exact
Update holiday hours and make sure your phone and WhatsApp number work.

## 5. Post updates
Use Google Posts for recent events, offers and seasonal packages.

> Consistency beats perfection. A few minutes a week keeps your profile active.
